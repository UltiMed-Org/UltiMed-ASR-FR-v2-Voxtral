# The French voices cleared for UltiMed v2 generation. Ten voices, ten distinct
# identities, five female and five male.

# A voice is heard as its SECOND name (the acoustic donor); the first name is the
# semantic donor and moves pronunciation and stability only. So this list is ten
# different speakers, not ten variants of a few: fr_female, fr_male, ar_male, es_male,
# nl_female, de_female, it_male, pt_female, pt_male, cheerful_female.
#
# Selected in two listening rounds, and the second one mattered: round 1 (A8) cleared 12
# voices on FLEURS sentences, round 2 (A9) re-auditioned all 16 candidates on hard medical
# text and disagreed about 6 of them, dropping 4 of the 12 and promoting 2 of the 4
# marginals. FLEURS clearance does not predict medical clearance, which is the whole
# reason v2's voices were not simply inherited from A8. See PLAN A9 for the verdicts.
#
# Generated by voxtral/cloning/medical_audition.sh, judged by ear, via Claude Code.
