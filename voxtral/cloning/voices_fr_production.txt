# The French voices cleared for UltiMed v2 generation. Nine voices, nine distinct
# identities, four female and five male.
#
# A voice is heard as its SECOND name (the acoustic donor); the first name is the
# semantic donor and moves pronunciation and stability only. So this list is nine
# different speakers, not nine variants of a few: fr_female, fr_male, ar_male, es_male,
# nl_female, de_female, it_male, pt_female, pt_male.
#
# It cleared TEN, five female and five male. `h_frm_chf` (heard as cheerful_female) was
# dropped on 2026-10-15, which is why the roster is 4F/5M rather than even: in the
# unbiased 5% STT sample of the v2 run it put 14 of its 180 checked clips over the CER
# gate against 4 of 165 for the next worst voice and zero for four of the ten, p90 CER
# 0.195 against 0.014 to 0.036 for the rest, and every `loop` and `overrun` among the
# first 51 flagged clips was its. Its median CER was mid-pack (0.0069), so it read most
# sentences well and then lost the text entirely on some: it derailed rather than being
# bad, and the UltiMed code gate (utils/code_loops.py there) does catch those takes. It
# was dropped anyway, because a dataset is not improved by a voice that needs retrying.
# The acoustic donor may still be usable under a different semantic donor (h_frf_chf,
# h_itf_chf): the first name is what moves stability, and that pairing was never
# auditioned. Nothing was deleted server side, so re-adding a line is all it takes.
#
# Selected in two listening rounds, and the second one mattered: round 1 (A8) cleared 12
# voices on FLEURS sentences, round 2 (A9) re-auditioned all 16 candidates on hard medical
# text and disagreed about 6 of them, dropping 4 of the 12 and promoting 2 of the 4
# marginals. FLEURS clearance does not predict medical clearance, which is the whole
# reason v2's voices were not simply inherited from A8. See PLAN A9 for the verdicts.
